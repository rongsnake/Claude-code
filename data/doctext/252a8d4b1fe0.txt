Submission of Potential Deliverable Obligations and Challenges
The Credit Derivatives Determinations Committees Rules (the "DC Rules") contain provisions allowing
Eligible Market Participants to submit obligations for consideration by the Determinations Committee
("DC") for inclusion on the list of Deliverable Obligations for an auction, and also challenges to
obligations included on the Supplemental List (as defined in the DC Rules). Market participants should
review the DC Rules, which set out the procedures applicable to this process.
Proposed obligations (with the required documentation) and challenges may be submitted by sending
an email with all information required by the DC Rules to the following email address:
DeliverableObligations@isda.org
Please note that ISDA will only forward obligations to the DC for which the necessary information has
been provided.
