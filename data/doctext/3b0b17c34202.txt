Annex
ISDA Credit Derivatives Determinations Committee for the Americas
Statement regarding DC question number 2010032501, "Has a Bankruptcy Credit Event occurred
with respect to Ambac Assurance Corporation?"
The ISDA Credit Derivatives Determinations Committee for the Americas (the "DC") has resolved to
publish the following statement regarding DC question number 2010032501, "Has a Bankruptcy Credit
Event occurred with respect to Ambac Assurance Corporation?".
Any capitalized term used but not defined in this statement shall have the meaning given to such term in
the 2003 ISDA Credit Derivatives Definitions, as amended by the 2009 ISDA Credit Derivatives
Determinations Committees, Auction Settlement and Restructuring Supplement to the 2003 ISDA Credit
Derivatives Definitions published by the International Swaps and Derivatives Association, Inc. ("ISDA") on
July 14, 2009, or the Credit Derivatives Determinations Committee Rules published by ISDA on
December 18, 2009 (the "DC Rules")1.
On April 20, 2010, ISDA, on behalf of the DC, published a preliminary list of potential Deliverable
Obligations (the "Preliminary List")2 in preparation for the auction to be held for purposes of settling Credit
Derivatives Transactions referencing Ambac Assurance Corporation (the "Auction"). ISDA has not yet
been provided with the documentation required by Section 3.3(b) of the DC Rules for the majority of
obligations on the Preliminary List (including in particular the relevant financial guarantee insurance policy
documentation of Ambac Assurance Corporation, as well as offering documents and trust indentures or
pooling and servicing agreements).
The DC has therefore resolved to publish a revised version of the Preliminary List, indicating obligations
for which (a) all documentation is available, (b) some documentation is available (and which
documentation is missing) and (c) all documentation is missing. As required by Section 3.3(b) of the DC
Rules, only obligations for which the required documentation has been submitted may be included on the
Initial List of Deliverable Obligations, therefore those obligations appearing in categories (b) and (c) above
will NOT be included on the Initial List unless the relevant documentation becomes available.
A market participant who wishes to ensure that an obligation on the Preliminary List for which the
required documentation is currently missing is also included on the Initial List may provide ISDA
with the required documentation for such obligation by Friday, May 7. Any obligation for which
the required documentation is not received by ISDA on or prior to this date will not be included on
the Initial List. Please note that a party who submits an obligation after publication of the Initial
List may be subject to bearing the legal costs of reviewing the relevant documentation, as
described further in Sections 3.3(c) and 3.3(d) of the DC Rules.
Any required documentation may be sent to deliverableobligations@isda.org. Please do NOT submit any
non-public or confidential documentation to this address. When submitting documentation, please include
in the subject line the relevant CUSIP or ISIN number and the name(s) of the document(s) provided.
1 The DC Rules are available online at: http://www.isda.org/credit/docs/DC_Rules_December_2009_version.pdf.
2 The Preliminary List is available online at: http://www.isda.org/companies/ambac/ambac.html.
