Date: May 24, 2012
Determinations Committee: Americas
Meeting Date: May 24, 2012
DC Issue 2012051401: Has a Bankruptcy Credit Event occurred with
respect to Residential Capital, LLC?
Question for vote Do you approve and agree to publish the 2012
Residential Capital, LLC Credit Derivatives Auction
Settlement Terms in the form provided, and in doing
so, do you agree that a public comment period will
not apply to these 2012 Residential Capital, LLC
Credit Derivatives Auction Settlement Terms?
The changes compared to the Eastman Kodak ASTs
are as follows:
(a) to correct some DC Rules references;
(b) to change references to “Mod R or Mod Mod R”
to the formal terminology of ““Restructuring
Maturity Limitation and Fully Transferable
Obligation" or "Modified Restructuring Maturity
Limitation and Conditionally Transferable
Obligation””;
(c) to provide that where the amount calculated as the
Initial Market Midpoint falls equidistant between two
Relevant Pricing Increments, such amount will be
rounded up; and
(d) to change “Derivative” to “Derivatives” in
“Credit Derivative Auction Settlement Terms”.
