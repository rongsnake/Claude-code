EMEA Determinations Committee Statement
Anglo Irish Bank Corporation Limited
November 25, 2010.
The EMEA Determinations Committee resolved that CDS auctions would be held which apply to all
relevant senior and subordinated auction-settled CDS transactions in relation to Anglo Irish Bank
Corporation Limited.
In light of the timing constraints imposed by the pending exchange offer relating to certain Anglo
Irish Bank Corporation Limited subordinated bonds, it is anticipated that the auctions will be held on
an expedited timescale, probably in the week commencing 6th December.
