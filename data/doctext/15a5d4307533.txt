II. Ranges of Scheduled Termination Dates
It has been resolved that neither Senior Auctions nor Subordinated Auctions will be held in respect of
Bucket 4 and Bucket 5. If the Buyer exercises the Movement Option, the applicable Deliverable
Obligations will be settled through the applicable auction for Bucket 3. If the Seller exercises the
Movement Option, the applicable Deliverable Obligations will be settled through the applicable auction
for Bucket 6. The Movement Option Cut-off Date is Wednesday 27 July 2011, but ISDA would encourage
market participants intending to utilise the Movement Option to exercise it as soon as possible to avoid
operational difficulties.
