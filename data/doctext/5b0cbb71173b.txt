Annex
Americas Determinations Committee
Statement regarding DC question number 2010032501, "Has a Bankruptcy Credit Event occurred with
respect to Ambac Assurance Corporation?"
The ISDA Credit Derivatives Determinations Committee for the Americas (the "DC") has resolved to
publish the following statement regarding DC question number 2010032501, "Has a Bankruptcy Credit
Event occurred with respect to Ambac Assurance Corporation?" (the "DC Question").
Any capitalized term used and not defined in this statement shall have the meaning given to such term
in the 2003 ISDA Credit Derivatives Definitions, as amended by the July 2009 ISDA Credit Derivatives
Determinations Committees, Auction Settlement and Restructuring Supplement to the 2003 ISDA Credit
Derivatives Definitions.
The DC has previously resolved to hold an Auction for Ambac Assurance Corporation (the "Auction").
This statement is intended to provide further information regarding the next steps and timing for the
Auction.
The DC anticipates that there will be a large number of obligations that may qualify as Deliverable
Obligations for the Auction ("Potentially Deliverable Obligations"), and that preparation of the Initial List
of Deliverable Obligations will therefore require additional time compared to some other recent
auctions.
The timing of publication of the Initial List will be an important factor in determining the Auction Date.
Whilst a specific Auction Date has not yet been discussed, the DC currently anticipates holding the
Auction in mid-May.
As for previous credit event auctions that have involved a large number of Potentially Deliverable
Obligations, the DC has resolved to publish a non-binding, indicative list of Potentially Deliverable
Obligations (the "Preliminary List of Obligations"), to provide information to the market on the universe
of Potentially Deliverable Obligations under consideration by the DC. The DC will review all of the
obligations on the Preliminary List of Obligations to the extent that the relevant documentation is
available. If a market participant wishes a particular obligation on the Preliminary List of Obligations to
be included on the Final List, then it should provide publicly available documentation for such obligation
(including in particular the relevant Ambac policy documentation as well as the indenture, trust
agreement and any offering documents). Any publicly available documentation may be sent to
deliverableobligations@isda.org. Please do NOT submit any private or confidential documents to this
address.
Once the Initial List is published, market participants will have at least two days to submit any additional
obligations for inclusion on the Supplemental List. This procedure is contained in Section 3.3(c) of the
Determinations Committees Rules, which are available here.
