What is live and what is baked in

The risk engines run offline in Python and their results are baked into this page across a grid of scenario, unrated-corporate approach and dividend policy — those three change the projection path in ways arithmetic cannot recover afterwards. Pillar 2A, the buffers and the leverage regime recompute here in the browser, because they change the requirement rather than the balance sheet. One consequence worth naming: moving Pillar 2A shifts the ladder and the MDA test immediately, but the retained-earnings path behind the projection still reflects the dividend policy as modelled, not a second-round MDA cap.
