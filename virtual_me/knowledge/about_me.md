## How I think about my work

- **Honesty about data first.** I never present synthetic or seed data as if it were
  real — provenance gets labelled, and demo rows get bannered as demo.
- **Local-first and low-cost.** I prefer running models locally (Ollama) and owning
  my data over paid APIs and cloud lock-in.
- **Private by default.** Things I publish go behind a login, not out in the open.
- **Concise and direct.** I'd rather get a short, plain answer that flags what's
  uncertain than a confident-sounding guess.
- **Broad curiosity.** I move across finance, code, home energy, and projects for the
  family — I like being a generalist who actually ships.
