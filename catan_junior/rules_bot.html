A turn, in order

  1. Roll the die. Every player collects from the stockpile for their lairs touching a matching island.
  2. Trade at the market: 2 identical tiles → 1 tile of your choice, as often as you like.
  3. Build ships, lairs and Coco tiles — as many as you can pay for.

The Ghost Captain

Roll a 6 and nobody collects. Instead you move him to any other island, then take 2 tiles of that island's resource from the stockpile.

While he sits on an island it produces nothing, for anyone. He never steals from a player.

Winning

First player with 7 pirates' lairs on the board wins — you start with 2, so that's 5 more.

Hold the most Coco tiles and you park a lair on Spooky Island. It counts toward your 7 — but you lose it the moment someone matches or beats your Coco count.
